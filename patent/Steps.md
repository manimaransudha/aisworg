The steps we will follow
1. Define the invention. Identify the specific technical mechanism, how it works, what problem it solves, and how it differs from ordinary AI-agent orchestration. We will use your existing manuscript and architecture material rather than start from scratch.
2. Search existing inventions. Search Google Patents and WIPO PATENTSCOPE for similar systems, claims and technical mechanisms. Record the closest prior art and the differences. I can help conduct and analyse this search.
3. Assess patent eligibility and novelty. Compare the proposed mechanism against the prior art and the Indian legal requirements. Decide whether a patent application is worth pursuing. This is an assessment, not a guarantee of grant.
4. Prepare the patent documents. If proceeding, draft the title, field, background, summary, detailed description, drawings where needed, claims and abstract. Claims are particularly important because they define the requested protection. We must describe the invention sufficiently; a vague conceptual statement is not enough.
5. File directly with IP India. Create an account on the official patent e-filing portal(https://ipronline.ipindia.gov.in/epatentfiling/goForLogin/doLogin?utm_source=chatgpt.com). Select the appropriate application type and prepare the required forms, specification and fees. The official filing instructions (https://ipindia.gov.in/filing-process?utm_source=chatgpt.com) explain the process. 
6. Manage examination and deadlines. Track publication, request examination within the applicable deadline, respond to examination objections, and meet any subsequent requirements. Filing an application does not itself mean a patent will be granted.
