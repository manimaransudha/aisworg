Major implementation gaps:

- The selected alternative, the Decision-level rationale, assumptions and consequences have no stored field or edit path.

- Provenance columns are overwritten on every hop and the contributor and approval history has no read path.
- Modifications, version identity and Deliverable pinning to a Decision version do not exist.
- Evidence and Knowledge are alternatives in the creation rule, and their ids are not validated.
- The supersession comment is required and not stored.
- Reuse of Decisions across SEUs is not implemented.
