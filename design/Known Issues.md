# Known Issues

A running register of known defects and gaps that the owner has accepted for now. An entry is added when the owner confirms the issue is known. Each entry records what fails, where, how it shows up and what would close it. A fix that needs design work is raised as a Change Request in [Change Requests.md](design/Change%20Requests.md) and linked from the entry.

Status values: **Open** (known, accepted for now), **Planned** (tied to scheduled work), **Unconfirmed** (observed, cause not yet confirmed by the owner), **Closed** (fixed, kept for the record).
