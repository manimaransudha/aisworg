# Workbench

## Introduction

Part 2 specified **SEU Structure** — what exists within the AI Software Organisation: Objective, Capability, Service, Role, Participant, Activity, Artefact, Evidence, Knowledge, Decision, Governance. Part 3 specified **SEU Loops** — how those entities reason: individually through the Organisational Reasoning Cycle, collectively through the Capability Reasoning Network, and beyond any single SEU through Continuous Organisational Learning. None of these, on their own, explain how organisational intent actually becomes coordinated organisational action — how a single objective turns into work distributed across many capabilities, carried out by many participants, over an extended period of time.

This Part addresses that question directly, through the **Workbench**: the environment within which structure and reasoning are put to work. It introduces the Deliverable as the digital carrier of organisational intent as it moves through execution, and Workflow as the mechanism — layered into organisational, capability and participant levels — through which that movement is coordinated without collapsing the encapsulation each level depends on. It examines collaboration as it actually occurs, splitting the ephemeral exchange of conversation from the persistent record of evidence, knowledge and decision, and reframes responsibility not as a fixed chart but as a query derivable from the underlying structure. It closes by turning to how the organisation acquires understanding in the first place — recovering it from legacy systems whose original intent has partially disappeared, and creating it natively as new systems are built — understanding that Part 3's Continuous Organisational Learning has already shown how to turn into lasting engineering capital.

Where Part 2 asked what an organisation is made of, and Part 3 asked how it reasons, this Part asks how it works — and, having answered that, the manuscript is in a position to state, in the chapter that follows, the principles the entire model has been in service of.
