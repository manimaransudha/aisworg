# SEU Loops

## Introduction

Part 2 defined **SEU Structure** — the entities of which a Software Engineering Unit is built: Objective, Capability, Service, Role, Participant, Activity, Artefact, Evidence, Knowledge, Decision and Governance. These entities describe **what exists**. An organisation, however, cannot be understood merely by identifying its constituent entities; it must also be understood through its behaviour, which emerges from their continual interaction — activity creates artefact, artefact produces evidence, evidence supports knowledge, knowledge informs decision, and decision influences future activity in turn. The organisation therefore behaves as a continuously adapting system rather than a sequence of isolated engineering tasks.

This Part examines those behavioural relationships — the organisation's **SEU Loops** — beginning with the fundamental behavioural model of the AI Software Organisation: the **Organisational Reasoning Cycle**. It then extends that cycle from an individual capability to the organisation as a whole, through the **Capability Reasoning Network**, and finally beyond any single Software Engineering Unit, through **Continuous Organisational Learning** — the loop through which understanding earned locally becomes engineering capital held organisationally.
