3. Banking

There isn't one universally accepted equivalent of "PMBOK for banking."

But there are several strong sources:

IBM Banking Process and Service Models

IBM explicitly offers Banking Process and Service Models and Banking Data Warehouse / Industry Models.

BIAN

It provides a banking-specific service-domain model and standardized terminology for banking capabilities/services.

Assessment: ★★★★★ for a software-oriented Banking Domain Pack.

I would actually consider BIAN + IBM Banking Models + regulatory sources a stronger basis than trying to find a nonexistent single "Banking BoK."
