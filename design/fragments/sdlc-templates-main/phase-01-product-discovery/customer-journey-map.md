# Customer Journey Map
**Phase**: 01 - Product Discovery (aka: Discovery Sprint, Exploration Sprint, Problem/Solution Fit, Inception, Validation Sprint)
**Deliverable Type**: Customer Journey Map
**Template Purpose**: Visualize the end-to-end customer experience to identify opportunities and pain points
**Last Updated**: November 2025

## Template Overview

*A customer journey map traces the complete experience a customer has with your product, from initial awareness through long-term usage. It should include touchpoints, emotions, pain points, and opportunities at each stage. Use this map to identify moments that matter and optimize the customer experience.*

---

## Journey Overview: Enterprise Knowledge Worker

**Primary Persona**: Sarah Chen - Senior Marketing Manager
**Journey Scope**: From problem awareness to product advocacy (12-month timeline)
**Business Goal**: Convert enterprise prospects into successful, expanding customers

---

## Stage 1: Problem Recognition (Month 0)

### Context & Triggers
*Describe what causes the customer to recognize they have a problem that needs solving*

**Situation**: Sarah's marketing team is growing rapidly, and knowledge management is becoming chaotic
**Trigger Events**:
- New team member asks the same questions that were answered 3 months ago
- Critical campaign insights are lost when a team member leaves
- Team spends 2 hours in a meeting recreating a strategy that already exists somewhere

### Customer Actions
- Searches Google for "enterprise note sharing tools"
- Asks colleagues about knowledge management solutions
- Posts question in industry Slack communities
- Reviews current tools (Google Docs, Notion, Confluence) for limitations

### Touchpoints
- Industry blog posts and articles
- Peer recommendations and word-of-mouth
- Search engine results
- Social media discussions (LinkedIn, Twitter)

### Emotions & Mindset
**Feelings**: Frustrated, overwhelmed, concerned about team efficiency
**Thoughts**: "There has to be a better way to manage our team knowledge"
**Mindset**: Problem-aware but not yet solution-aware

### Pain Points
- Doesn't know what solutions exist
- Overwhelmed by number of tool options
- Uncertain about budget and approval process
- Worried about team adoption challenges

### Opportunities
- **Content Marketing**: Create helpful content about knowledge management best practices
- **SEO Optimization**: Rank for relevant search terms
- **Community Presence**: Participate in industry forums and communities
- **Thought Leadership**: Publish case studies and research on knowledge management ROI

## Journey Insights & Optimization Opportunities

### Critical Moments That Matter
1. **First Demo Experience**: Make or break moment for vendor consideration
2. **IT Security Review**: Technical validation that can derail or accelerate process
3. **Team First Use**: Initial user experience determines adoption success
4. **Early Value Realization**: First measurable benefit drives continued usage
5. **Expansion Decision**: Success with initial team drives organizational growth

### Emotional Journey Patterns
- **Frustration → Hope**: Problem recognition to solution discovery
- **Caution → Confidence**: Evaluation to purchase decision
- **Anxiety → Relief**: Implementation to successful adoption
- **Satisfaction → Advocacy**: Value realization to expansion

### Key Optimization Areas
1. **Reduce Evaluation Friction**: Simplify comparison and trial processes
2. **Accelerate Time-to-Value**: Focus on quick wins during onboarding
3. **Proactive Success Management**: Anticipate challenges and provide solutions
4. **Expansion Readiness**: Identify and nurture expansion opportunities early

### Success Metrics by Stage
- **Awareness**: Brand recognition and consideration rates
- **Evaluation**: Demo-to-trial and trial-to-purchase conversion
- **Onboarding**: Time-to-first-value and user adoption rates
- **Usage**: Feature adoption and customer satisfaction scores
- **Expansion**: Account growth and customer lifetime value
- **Advocacy**: Reference participation and referral rates
