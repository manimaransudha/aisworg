## Executive Summary

*Provide a 2-3 sentence overview of key JTBD insights and their strategic implications.*

Enterprise knowledge workers hire note-sharing solutions primarily to preserve and access critical information that drives decision-making, with secondary jobs around collaboration and knowledge transfer. The core functional job is "help me capture and retrieve the right information when I need it," while emotional jobs center on reducing anxiety about losing important insights and increasing confidence in decision-making.

## Jobs-to-be-Done Framework Overview

*Explain the JTBD methodology and how insights were gathered.*

### Research Methodology
- **Customer Interviews**: 45 interviews with enterprise knowledge workers across 6 industries
- **Observational Studies**: Shadowing sessions with 12 teams during knowledge-intensive work
- **Survey Data**: 300+ responses from target customer segments
- **Win/Loss Analysis**: Analysis of 25 competitive evaluations and purchase decisions

### JTBD Analysis Framework
- **Functional Jobs**: What customers are trying to accomplish
- **Emotional Jobs**: How customers want to feel during the process
- **Social Jobs**: How customers want to be perceived by others
- **Job Context**: Circumstances that trigger the need for a solution

## Primary Jobs-to-be-Done

### Job #1: Preserve Critical Information for Future Access

**Job Statement**: *"When I encounter important information during my work, I want to capture and organize it effectively so that I can retrieve it quickly when making future decisions."*

**Customer Segments**: All knowledge workers, especially senior individual contributors and managers

**Job Context**:
- During meetings with important decisions or insights
- When researching solutions to complex problems
- While reviewing documents with key findings
- When learning new processes or methodologies

**Current Solutions & Pain Points**:
- **Email to self**: Hard to search, gets buried in inbox
- **Sticky notes**: Easy to lose, not searchable, not shareable
- **Word documents**: Version control issues, hard to organize
- **Existing tools**: Too complex, don't integrate with workflow

**Success Criteria**:
- Information captured in <30 seconds
- Retrieved in <10 seconds when needed
- 95% confidence that nothing important is lost
- Accessible from any device or location

**Opportunity Score**: 9.2/10 (High importance, low satisfaction with current solutions)

### Job #2: Share Knowledge Effectively with Team Members

**Job Statement**: *"When I have valuable insights or information, I want to share it with relevant team members in a way that's easy for them to understand and act upon."*

**Customer Segments**: Team leads, project managers, subject matter experts

**Job Context**:
- Onboarding new team members
- Transferring knowledge before leaving a project
- Sharing research findings or best practices
- Documenting lessons learned from projects

**Current Solutions & Pain Points**:
- **Email**: Gets lost, hard to update, poor discoverability
- **Slack/Teams**: Information gets buried in chat history
- **Wiki systems**: Too formal, time-consuming to maintain
- **Meetings**: Information not retained, scheduling overhead

**Success Criteria**:
- Shared information is discoverable by relevant people
- Updates and changes are automatically communicated
- Recipients can easily understand context and relevance
- Sharing process takes minimal time and effort

**Opportunity Score**: 8.7/10 (High importance, moderate satisfaction gaps)

### Job #3: Build on Previous Work and Avoid Duplication

**Job Statement**: *"When starting a new project or solving a problem, I want to quickly find and build upon previous work so that I don't waste time recreating existing solutions."*

**Customer Segments**: Individual contributors, consultants, researchers

**Job Context**:
- Beginning new projects or initiatives
- Solving problems similar to past challenges
- Creating proposals or recommendations
- Conducting research on familiar topics

**Current Solutions & Pain Points**:
- **File systems**: Hard to search, unclear what's current
- **Google Drive/SharePoint**: Poor search, unclear ownership
- **Asking colleagues**: Interrupts others, incomplete information
- **Starting from scratch**: Wastes time, misses opportunities

**Success Criteria**:
- Relevant previous work surfaces automatically
- Clear understanding of what's current and applicable
- Easy to build upon existing work without disruption
- Confidence that all relevant information is considered

**Opportunity Score**: 8.4/10 (High importance, significant satisfaction gaps)

## Secondary Jobs-to-be-Done

### Job #4: Demonstrate Expertise and Contribution

**Job Statement**: *"When working with colleagues and stakeholders, I want to showcase my knowledge and contributions so that I'm recognized as a valuable team member."*

**Job Type**: Social Job  
**Customer Segments**: Individual contributors seeking career advancement

**Success Criteria**:
- Contributions are visible to relevant stakeholders
- Expertise is discoverable when others need help
- Credit is maintained for original insights and work
- Professional reputation is enhanced through knowledge sharing

**Opportunity Score**: 7.1/10 (Moderate importance, moderate satisfaction)

### Job #5: Reduce Anxiety About Forgetting Important Information

**Job Statement**: *"When I encounter important information, I want to be confident it's safely stored and retrievable so that I don't worry about forgetting critical details."*

**Job Type**: Emotional Job  
**Customer Segments**: Detail-oriented professionals, compliance-focused roles

**Success Criteria**:
- Peace of mind that information is safely captured
- Confidence in ability to retrieve information when needed
- Reduced mental load from trying to remember everything
- Trust in the system's reliability and permanence

**Opportunity Score**: 8.9/10 (High emotional importance, low satisfaction with current solutions)

## Job Context Analysis

*Analyze the circumstances and triggers that activate these jobs.*

### High-Frequency Contexts

**Daily Work Situations** (Multiple times per day):
- Taking notes during meetings
- Researching solutions to immediate problems
- Reviewing and commenting on documents
- Responding to questions from colleagues

**Weekly Work Situations** (1-3 times per week):
- Planning new projects or initiatives
- Conducting deeper research on complex topics
- Preparing presentations or reports
- Onboarding or training team members

**Monthly Work Situations** (1-4 times per month):
- Quarterly planning and review sessions
- Knowledge transfer during role transitions
- Compliance audits and documentation reviews
- Cross-team collaboration on major initiatives

### Contextual Factors That Influence Job Performance

**Time Pressure**: When under deadline pressure, users prioritize speed over organization
**Collaboration Intensity**: More collaborative projects increase sharing and discovery needs
**Information Complexity**: Technical or nuanced information requires better organization
**Team Stability**: High turnover increases knowledge preservation and transfer needs
**Regulatory Environment**: Compliance requirements add documentation and audit needs

## Competitive Job Analysis

*How customers currently hire different solutions for these jobs.*

### Current Solution Landscape

**For Information Capture**:
- **Primary**: Email, note-taking apps (Apple Notes, Google Keep)
- **Secondary**: Word documents, physical notebooks
- **Satisfaction**: Low (fragmented, hard to organize)

**For Knowledge Sharing**:
- **Primary**: Email, Slack/Teams, informal conversations
- **Secondary**: Wiki systems, shared documents
- **Satisfaction**: Moderate (works but inefficient)

**For Information Discovery**:
- **Primary**: Google search, asking colleagues, file system search
- **Secondary**: Company wikis, documentation systems
- **Satisfaction**: Low (time-consuming, incomplete results)

### Switching Triggers

**What causes customers to fire current solutions**:
- Lost important information due to poor organization
- Spent excessive time searching for existing information
- Team member left and took critical knowledge with them
- Compliance audit revealed documentation gaps
- New team member struggled to find relevant information

## Product Strategy Implications

*How JTBD insights should influence product development and positioning.*

### Core Product Priorities

**1. Effortless Capture** (Job #1 Priority)
- One-click capture from any application or browser
- Smart categorization and tagging
- Mobile-first capture experience
- Integration with existing workflow tools

**2. Intelligent Discovery** (Jobs #1 & #3 Priority)
- AI-powered search across all organizational knowledge
- Contextual recommendations based on current work
- Automatic surfacing of relevant previous work
- Smart notifications for related information

**3. Seamless Sharing** (Job #2 Priority)
- Context-aware sharing with relevant team members
- Automatic updates when shared information changes
- Permission management that follows organizational structure
- Integration with communication tools

### Feature Development Framework

**Must-Have Features** (Address primary functional jobs):
- Universal capture interface
- Intelligent search and discovery
- Real-time collaboration and sharing
- Mobile accessibility

**Should-Have Features** (Address emotional and social jobs):
- Contribution tracking and attribution
- Knowledge expertise profiles
- Confidence indicators for information reliability
- Usage analytics and insights

**Could-Have Features** (Nice-to-have enhancements):
- Advanced visualization and mind mapping
- Integration with specialized industry tools
- Custom workflow automation
- Advanced analytics and reporting

### Go-to-Market Messaging

**Primary Value Proposition**: "Never lose important information again"
- Addresses Job #1 (preservation) and Job #5 (anxiety reduction)
- Resonates with universal pain point across all segments

**Secondary Messages**:
- "Build on your team's collective knowledge" (Jobs #2 & #3)
- "Become your organization's knowledge expert" (Job #4)
- "Reduce time spent searching for information" (Job #3)

- Team knowledge coverage scores

**Job #3 - Building on Previous Work**:
- Reduction in duplicate work (measure through content similarity)
- Time to find relevant previous work (<2 minutes)
- Project startup time reduction (40% target)

### Leading Indicators

- Daily active capture events per user
- Search success rates and query refinement patterns
- Sharing and collaboration engagement metrics
- Mobile usage patterns and offline access frequency
