## Template Explanation
*This document uses the RACI framework (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) to clarify roles and responsibilities for key decisions and activities in the product development process. Use this template to eliminate confusion, ensure accountability, and streamline decision-making across cross-functional teams.*

## RACI Framework Definition

### Role Definitions
*[Template: Define what each RACI designation means]*

**Responsible (R)**: The person(s) who perform the work and are responsible for completing the task
**Accountable (A)**: The person who is ultimately answerable for the decision or task completion (only one A per task)
**Consulted (C)**: People who provide input and expertise before decisions are made or work is completed
**Informed (I)**: People who need to be kept informed about progress and decisions but don't actively participate

## Escalation Matrix

### Decision Escalation Paths
*[Template: Define escalation procedures]*

**Level 1 - Team Level**:
- Technical decisions: Dev → EM
- Design decisions: Designer → DM
- Product decisions: PM → CPO
- Quality decisions: QA → QA Lead

**Level 2 - Department Level**:
- Cross-team conflicts: EM/DM/PM → CPO/CTO
- Resource allocation: Team Leads → Department Heads
- Timeline conflicts: PM/EM → CPO/CTO

**Level 3 - Executive Level**:
- Strategic decisions: CPO/CTO → CEO
- Budget decisions: Department Heads → CEO
- Customer escalations: VP CS → CEO
- Legal/Compliance: Legal → CEO

### Conflict Resolution
*[Template: Define conflict resolution process]*

**Step 1 - Direct Resolution**: Involved parties attempt direct resolution (24 hours)
**Step 2 - Manager Mediation**: Immediate managers facilitate resolution (48 hours)
**Step 3 - Department Escalation**: Department heads make binding decision (72 hours)
**Step 4 - Executive Decision**: C-level executives make final decision (96 hours)

## Governance Review & Updates

### RACI Maintenance
*[Template: Define how RACI chart will be maintained]*

**Review Frequency**: Quarterly or when organizational changes occur
**Review Participants**: All department heads and key stakeholders
**Update Process**: 
1. Identify gaps or conflicts in current RACI
2. Propose changes with stakeholder input
3. Review and approve changes with affected parties
4. Communicate updates to all teams
5. Update documentation and training materials

**Success Metrics**:
- Decision-making speed and clarity
- Reduced escalations and conflicts
- Stakeholder satisfaction with role clarity
- Process efficiency improvements
