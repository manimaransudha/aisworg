## Customer Pain Points Analysis

### Top Customer Complaints

#### 1. Mobile App Limitations (mentioned in 34% of negative feedback)
**Issue**: Mobile app lacks feature parity with web version
**Impact**: 23% of users report frustration with mobile experience
**Specific Complaints**:
- "Can't access advanced formatting options on mobile"
- "File uploads often fail on mobile, have to switch to desktop"
- "Offline mode doesn't sync properly when back online"

**Customer Quotes**:
> "I love the desktop version, but the mobile app feels like an afterthought. Need better mobile support for remote work."
> 
> "Mobile app crashes when I try to open large documents. This is a dealbreaker for field work."

#### 2. Performance Issues During Peak Hours (mentioned in 28% of negative feedback)
**Issue**: Slower response times during 2-4 PM EST peak usage
**Impact**: 15% of users report productivity impact
**Specific Complaints**:
- "System gets sluggish during lunch hours when everyone is online"
- "Search takes forever during busy times"
- "Collaboration features lag when multiple people are editing"

**Customer Quotes**:
> "Performance is great most of the time, but becomes frustrating during peak hours when we need it most."

#### 3. Limited Integration Options (mentioned in 22% of negative feedback)
**Issue**: Missing integrations with popular business tools
**Impact**: 18% of users request specific integrations
**Most Requested Integrations**:
- Slack (mentioned 12 times)
- Microsoft Teams (mentioned 9 times)
- Salesforce (mentioned 7 times)
- Google Workspace (mentioned 6 times)
- Jira (mentioned 5 times)

**Customer Quotes**:
> "We need Slack integration to make this part of our daily workflow. Right now it feels disconnected."
> 
> "Would love to create notes directly from Salesforce opportunities. Manual copying is inefficient."

#### 4. Advanced Permission Management (mentioned in 19% of negative feedback)
**Issue**: Need more granular permission controls
**Impact**: 12% of enterprise customers request enhanced permissions
**Specific Requests**:
- Department-level access controls
- Time-based access permissions
- Read-only sharing with external users
- Audit trail for permission changes
