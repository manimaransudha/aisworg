# Summarise

## One para lay-summary

Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly capable of performing software engineering tasks, but current approaches continue to treat AI primarily as a tool coordinated through prompts, workflows and agent orchestration frameworks. This paper argues that the greater challenge is not building more capable AI systems but redesigning the organisations within which they operate. It proposes an Executable Organisational Operating Model (EOOM) in which organisational constructs such as authority, accountability, governance, evidence and organisational knowledge become computationally enforceable rather than remaining documented practices. By distinguishing the execution of an organisation from the execution of a workflow, the paper presents a new perspective on how software engineering organisations can safely integrate autonomous AI participants while preserving governance, continuity and organisational learning.

## Biography - long 

Sudha Manimaran is an independent IT consultant and researcher based in Chennai, India. She holds a Master of Engineering in Industrial Engineering from the College of Engineering, Guindy. Prior to establishing her independent consulting practice in 2016, she spent nearly two decades in the IT industry, including leadership roles at Accenture, Fidelity Investments, Wipro, HCL Perot Systems and Tata Consultancy Services, where she led large-scale software delivery, systems integration and organisational transformation initiatives. Her current research focuses on AI-native software engineering, organisational governance and executable organisational operating models that enable the integration of autonomous AI participants into software engineering organisations.
