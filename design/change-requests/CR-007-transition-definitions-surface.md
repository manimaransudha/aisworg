# CR-007 — Transition Definitions as a manageable, visible authority surface (foundation for CR-006)

**Raised:** 2026-08-13 · **Origin:** Chapter 1 §18 authority deep-dive · **Status:** ✅ Closed 2026-08-13 — Step 1 (visible surface) + Step 2 (add/retire management) built; Step 3 (four tabs) delivered via CR-006 Stage 1b; reseed collision reconciled.

> **One deferred refinement (recorded, not blocking closure):** the retired-edge **traversal** semantics — grandfathering an SEU whose creation predates `retired_at`, and excluding retired edges in `findPossibleNextStates`/`find` — are deferred because they also involve **Template/Profile cleanup**. The `retired_at` column is in place for that later work. (Further out: per-transition **SLA** → stuck entity raises an Attention Item → flows through.)

### Why
Transition Definitions are the **data home** for the governed-transition wiring — each `transition_definitions` row already carries `required_authority_rule_id` (→ the authority rule → `required_badge_type`), plus policies/quality-gates. They are how authority is attached to a transition as *config*, not code. But today the **Transition Definition Authoring** surface only lists your authoring *drafts* (`deliverablesDB.findByCategory` — the authoring documents), and never shows or lets you manage the **live** `transition_definitions` rows. You can't make transition definitions the source of truth for authority if you can't see or edit the current set. This is the **configuration half of CR-006** — do it first.
